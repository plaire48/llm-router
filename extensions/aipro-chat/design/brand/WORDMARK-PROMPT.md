# Nubi Korean wordmark

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The approved bird is the shape and palette reference. The first heavy wordmark was rejected for missing the bird's light personality. This revision softens and lifts the Korean letter terminals and uses restrained blue-teal. The user finalized Korean rather than the explored Latin option.

Original: `nubi-wordmark-master.png`. `scripts/icons.mjs` trims transparent padding and downsizes it to `public/icons/nubi-wordmark.webp` without redrawing or recoloring it.

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: refined Korean custom logotype for Nubi, alpha-transparent PNG.
Input: approved beakless teal chibi bird mascot, COLOR and PERSONALITY reference only. Do not draw the bird.
Primary request: a sophisticated characterful wordmark reading exactly "누비", two correct Hangul syllables 누 and 비. Match this small bird's LIGHT, curious, clever, warm personality. Think a tiny bird quietly hopping and a softly folded feather. It should feel airy, poised and gently alive, not heavy block type or a toy brand.
Letterform design: custom medium-bold Korean sans lettering, visibly slimmer strokes than a heavy rounded font, generous open counters, gently softened corners rather than fully sausage-rounded bars. Low-key organic modulation in the strokes. Slight asymmetry and the most subtle upward lift at the end of the ㄴ in 누, echoing the folded wing. Keep the conventional clean geometry of 누 and 비 perfectly legible. Let the upright of 비 have a softly tapered, gently curved feather-like terminal. Consistent baseline, quiet rhythm, excellent optical spacing between the two syllables, professional modern enterprise usability. Balanced horizontal width, avoid stretched 누. Not handwriting or brush script. No novelty symbol substitutions.
Color: a VERY subtle deep teal to blue-teal gradient #216D80 to #277C94. Same color family as the bird's shaded underside. Restrained and dark enough on pale blue #EAF3FE, no saturated royal blue, no neon, no pale unreadable lettering.
Output: one horizontal wordmark only, visually centered and occupying 80% width of a transparent landscape 2:1 canvas. True alpha transparency. Flat crisp lettering without bevels or texture. No bird, extra words, latin text, tagline, glow, face, eyes, tiny sparkle, underline, border or background plate.
Text verbatim: "누비".
Avoid: massive puffy strokes, bubbly cartoon letters, mechanical squared blocks, overly cute baby-product typography, generic default font, stiff identical radii.
```
