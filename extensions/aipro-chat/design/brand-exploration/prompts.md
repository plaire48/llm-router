# Brand concept prompts

Tool: built-in image_gen. Initial generation followed by one targeted background/legibility edit. Preview exploration only.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: ONE polished landscape brand exploration board comparing THREE proposed identities for an enterprise Korean browser AI Agent. This is a concept presentation for choosing a new brand, not finished vector assets. Generate a wide high-resolution image, approximately 1536x1024.
Product: a friendly capable browser companion that reads webpages, understands DOM, fills forms, clicks, navigates, finds images and automates work. It connects multiple LLM providers; its own identity must be independent. User specifically wants deformed/chibi proportions, simple memorable forms and expressive potential, refined enough for enterprise work.
Art direction: Korean CX product sensibility, very pale blue/lavender/mint atmosphere, white surfaces, deep ink #191F28 typography. Accent #3182F6 blue, secondary #7B61E6 violet and #189BB0 teal. Soft satin 2.5D surface with VERY subtle volume, not glossy candy or glass. One-piece friendly mascot/symbol silhouettes with generous negative space. No robot antenna, no gears, no brain, no sparkle AI star, no chatbot speech bubble, no detailed arms or legs. Avoid copying existing AI provider logos. Do not include any AI Pro, OpenAI, FabriX or other company logo.
Composition: a clean editorial brand board with THREE equal spacious columns, almost-white background with extremely subtle cool tints, fine divisions, plenty of breathing room. Each column contains one large main icon, a crisp wordmark below, the Korean name underneath, then a small flat simplified rendering of the SAME icon at toolbar scale next to its wordmark. Minimal text. No thick card borders. Keep baseline and icon positions strictly aligned across the three columns. Show strong distinct silhouettes, not three identical mascots. All icons isolated cleanly from their backdrop.
LEFT concept, recommended: TABLU / 탭루. Invent an original friendly rounded browser-tab creature: oversized softly squared blue head/body with one broad offset browser-tab bump on its upper-left contour; a very small folded lower-right contour suggesting a page corner. The head accounts for nearly the whole silhouette, no separate limbs. Two bold simple white vertical oval eyes looking attentively to the right. Blue body, subtle violet at lower edge, no outline, no mouth or blush. The outer silhouette should immediately evoke a browser tab and a helpful companion. Slight forward lean, purposeful curious energy. Its compact flat version must be instantly readable at tiny scale.
CENTER concept: GYEOT / 곁. An original abstract companion symbol: two thick pill/pebble forms gently leaning toward each other, one larger violet form and one smaller soft-blue form, forming a subtle open doorway in negative space. Warm, calm and connected. NO face, no eyes, no limbs. It should work as both a corporate monogram-like mark and a warm animated pair. Distinct from left concept.
RIGHT concept: NUBOM / 누봄. An original rounded asymmetric teal pebble/bird-like scout mascot, gently tilted as though peeking around the edge of a browser. The body is short and plump, two dark ink oval eyes grouped near the upper-right area and a single small folded wing shape cut into the side; no beak, no legs. The silhouette should suggest looking around and exploring. Deliberately different from a browser tab. Understated delight, neither childish nor over-decorated.
Text must be EXACT and only: "TABLU", "탭루", "GYEOT", "곁", "NUBOM", "누봄". The wordmarks may repeat once in the tiny toolbar sample. Use excellent humanist geometric sans-serif typography, dark ink, medium-bold, tight balanced optical spacing; Korean compatible with Noto Sans KR. No subtitles, explanations, annotations, watermarks, slogans or decorative text.
Treat this as professional custom product identity exploration: beautiful restrained composition, memorable scalable marks, a touch of character, not a stock icon catalog.
